# D0-B3-A1-F1 — ELSEWHERE architectural order and rhythmic color

Scope: VOL. I / ELSEWHERE. Baseline: `dev@371ee4d079ccfaa39fb0add8bd62e60f55672562`. BSD-3-Clause. Deliver to `origin/dev` without a release or deployment.

Use the existing octagonal profile and M0 materials. The global ring grid is `4200 + 3i`; a full module is 12 rings (36 units). Primary rings and edge ribbons are owned by their world chunk, not by a local index reset. Align metal ceiling beams to the full module. Mount pairs of opaque equipment in the middle of every other eligible straight module, with a consistent equipment type for each long chapter zone. Use shell cutbacks with beveled returns and backing surfaces, not an offset model over an intact wall. Keep corners, gates, the pickup and junction entry and exit clear. No scattered ceiling lights, illuminated beam ornaments, freestanding equipment, cargo, or collision for scenery.

Lighting zones are fixed half-open world intervals: structural-only, continuous-edge, ceiling-pair and ordered-rhythm. Quiet and edge zones have no independent ceiling fixtures. The two fixture zones use symmetric recessed slots of identical dimensions and orientation; use a 12-unit spacing and 6-unit recess length. The ordered zone uses two fixed group IDs for a traveling brightness wave. It remains single-color during normal play. Debug can add a muted hue offset to one group only in the ordered zone. Standard and Sightseeing share grid, model dimensions and spacing; zones are re-fitted to each mode's fixed reference stations. Neither Boost nor music time can move a fixture.

The original six hues are anchors in an authored 33-cue score. Interpolate hue along the shortest circular arc, `delta = mod(h1 - h0 + 1.5, 1) - 0.5`, using quintic ease `6x^5 - 15x^4 + 10x^3` over each transition window. One current hue feeds the rim, ribbons, ordered slots, glass and fog; leave the neutral environment fill steady. The live amplitude is `I0 * (1 + a * energy * G + c * B)` with `G = exp(-5 * (1 - cos(2πb)))` and `B = (1 - cos(2πb/q))/2`. Use four beats generally and eight in INTRO and BREAK, with a two-beat continuous blend of section energy and breath. Reduced motion removes the short pulse and traveling wave while retaining illumination and the slowly changing hue. Neither the simulation clock nor the glass shader is modified.

Debug uses the same graphics and default lighting calculation as play. Provide fixed comparisons for each chapter, the paired and ordered zones, the junction and its routes, Sightseeing and Boost. Allow playing and scrubbing the score at a fixed station, plus a clearly labeled single/dual comparison in the ordered zone. Keep existing previews and unified audio tools.

Compare the baseline and candidate at the same fixed positions, camera, browser and quality; measure CPU/GPU timings, draw counts, stream lifetimes and static/lamp counts. Verify dark image distribution, wall backing and UV continuity, fixed world positions, normal/debug parity, context restoration, CPU/GPU gate parity, whole runs and unchanged pixels for the other volumes. Maintain the existing dark mood without raising global exposure, Bloom, DPR, adding textures or adding physical lights.
